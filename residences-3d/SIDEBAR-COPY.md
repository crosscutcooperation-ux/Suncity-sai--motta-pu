# Sidebar introduction and colour reference

The sidebar follows the user's Sai World Dream example: “Explore Sai Sun City,” the heading “The development,” and “Select a tower to explore its floors and apartments.” This replaces the location and brochure quotation with a direct explanation of the viewer.

Source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZVApjTAIm6sowDQDQqtmQgwwEQ6Qy3DJ/view

The interface palette follows the existing local Sai World Dream project: warm ivory `#f6f4ed`, green text `#304034`, muted text `#828576`, sage `#e7ebdf`, and forest-green floor-plan buttons `#3e5842`. This replaces the Sun City-specific navy button overrides while retaining its supplied brand logo and the neutral building/plan selection screens.
